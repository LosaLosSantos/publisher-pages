Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Data

Only data about TikTok accounts connected by the tool's owner through TikTok's login:

  • the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok;
  • the account's TikTok identifier, display name and granted permissions;
  • the creator information TikTok returns before a post;
  • the videos the owner selects, and the identifiers and status of the resulting posts.

The tool never receives a password: the login happens on TikTok's own pages.

Use

Only to send content the owner has confirmed to the owner's TikTok account and to show its status.

Storage

On the owner's computer only, with tokens encrypted. Nothing is shared or sold, and there is no advertising or analytics.

Removal

Disconnecting the account in the tool revokes the access at TikTok and deletes the tokens. The access can also be removed at any time from TikTok's app settings.

TikTok

What is sent to TikTok is handled under TikTok's Privacy Policy.
