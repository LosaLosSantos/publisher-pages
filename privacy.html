Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Data

Only data about the YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts its owner connects:

  • the access and refresh tokens issued by Google, Meta and TikTok;
  • each account's identifier, name and granted permissions;
  • what the platforms return before a post (for TikTok, the creator information; for Instagram, the publishing limit);
  • the videos the owner selects, and the identifiers, links and status of the resulting posts.

The tool never receives a password: logins happen on the platforms' own pages, and the Instagram token is generated in Meta's own dashboard.

Use

Only to publish the videos the owner chooses on the owner's own accounts, and to show their status. Each video and its text go only to the platform the owner picks. For Instagram, Meta downloads the video from a temporary signed link that expires after six hours; that link may be served through a tunnel or hosting provider.

Storage

On a computer the owner controls. Tokens are encrypted; videos and post records stay there until the owner deletes them. Data obtained from YouTube (the channel name, the status of uploaded videos) is refreshed at least every 30 days while the tool runs, and as soon as it starts again after a pause. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or analytics.

Removal

Disconnecting an account in the tool revokes the access where the platform allows it and deletes the tokens. Access can also be removed at any time from the platforms: for Google, from the Google security settings; for Instagram and TikTok, from their apps' settings.

Platforms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. What is sent to each platform is handled under its own policy: the Google Privacy Policy, the Meta Privacy Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy.
